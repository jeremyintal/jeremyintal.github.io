Personal-use integration

Jeremy Health Coach

A private, read-only connection between the owner's Google Health records and a personal wellness-coaching workflow on their Mac.

Purpose

The integration imports recent activity, sleep, and selected health measurements for personal trend review. The owner can use these records in an OpenAI Codex conversation to discuss training, recovery, and everyday habits.

This is a personal project maintained by jeremyintal, not a public sign-up service or a product operated by Google, Fitbit, or OpenAI.

Data access and use

Google authorization is requested for three read-only categories: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep. The current importer retrieves steps, exercise, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, weight, sleep, oxygen saturation, and respiratory rate. It does not request location or write permissions.

Downloaded records are stored in the owner's local project. When the owner requests AI-assisted coaching, selected records or summaries are sent to OpenAI for that task. Coaching is therefore not entirely offline. Storage, sharing, retention, and deletion are described in the privacy notice.

Limits

Wearable measurements and AI-generated interpretations can be incomplete or inaccurate. This project is for general wellness, not diagnosis, treatment, clinical monitoring, or emergency care. It does not replace a qualified health professional.

This public information site

These pages explain the integration. They contain no health records, account credentials, sign-in form, or live connection to Google Health.
